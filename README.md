# Busymate AI

Your mate for your business: an AI assistant that answers from your content, works with your connected systems, and hands conversations to your team.

[Explore Busymate AI](https://busymate.ai) · [Try it](https://busymate.ai/try) · [Documentation](https://busymate.ai/docs) · [Pricing](https://busymate.ai/pricing)

## Get started

1. Visit [busymate.ai/try](https://busymate.ai/try) and introduce your business.
2. Review your assistant and its knowledge in the Console.
3. Configure the channels and integrations available for your workspace.

## What this repository is for

This is the public home for product information, support, feature requests and community discussion. Product source and internal development are maintained separately in a private repository.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/serebano/busymate-ai/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/serebano/busymate-ai/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- [Ask a question or share an idea](https://github.com/serebano/busymate-ai/discussions).
- [Download the desktop app](https://busymate.ai/desktop).

Availability varies by integration. Consult the current product documentation and Console for supported capabilities.

## Support and privacy

Public issues and discussions are visible to everyone. Share reproducible steps and redacted screenshots; keep account credentials, customer conversations and personal data out of public reports. For account-specific help, use [Contact](https://busymate.ai/contact) or email [hi@busymate.ai](mailto:hi@busymate.ai).
